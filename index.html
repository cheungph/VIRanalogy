SF Delivery Analogy (順豐速遞世界)

Analogy Model 比喻模型
🏢 Hub 倉庫 = Cell 電池 🏃 Courier 速遞員 = Electric Charge 電荷 📦 Parcel 包裹 = Energy (Voltage 電壓) 🚧 Muddy Road 泥濘路 = Resistance 電阻
Analogy Insight: Couriers are never consumed or destroyed! They simply receive parcels at the warehouse, navigate the difficult road, drop off energy at the house, and return empty to loop continuously.

Real Electric Circuit (真實電路世界)

Flow: Positive (+) ➔ Negative (−)
🔋 Cell 乾電池 = Energy Source 🔵 Charge 正電荷 (+q) = Recycled Carrier ✨ Halo 光圈 = Energy (Voltage 電壓) 🛑 Resistor 電阻器 = Resistance 電阻
Physical Truth: Electric charges are never used up or lost! They leave the positive (+) terminal with electrical energy, pass through the resistor, give up energy at the bulb, and return to the negative (−) terminal.

How to Measure: Authentic Hong Kong Lab Metaphors (如何測量：魚蛋串與夾公仔)

Visualizing why an Ammeter must be connected in Series, while a Voltmeter must be connected in Parallel across two conjunctions.

🍢

Ammeter (安培計)

Measures Current (電流) in Ampere (A)

SERIES (串聯)
🍢 The Curry Fishball Metaphor (咖哩魚蛋串):

To skewer fishballs onto a bamboo stick, each fishball must be threaded onto the same line one after another.

Why disconnect / break the wire? (為何必須截斷電線？)

Current is the flow of electric charges. To count every single charge that passes, you must break open the circuit and insert the Ammeter directly into the wire path so 100% of charges pass through it.

Circuit Symbol: ───( A )─── (One unbroken loop / 單一迴路)
🕹️

Voltmeter (伏特計)

Measures Voltage (電壓) in Volt (V)

PARALLEL (並聯)
🕹️ The Claw Machine Metaphor (夾公仔機):

Two claws reach down from above to grip across the two sides of a doll without disturbing the prize floor.

Why DO NOT disconnect the wire? (為何絕不切斷電線？)

Voltage measures the energy difference across two sides of a component. You keep the original circuit intact, branch two test leads, and touch the two junctions!

Circuit Symbol: ●───( V )───● (Two junction nodes / 跨接兩端節點)

Three Fundamental Electrical Properties (三大電學物理量)

Exact Hong Kong Junior Secondary Integrated Science curriculum definitions. No abstract formula symbols (VIR)!

🌊 Unit: Ampere / 安培 (A)

Current (電流)

The flow of electric charges, flowing from the positive terminal of the cell to the negative terminal.

SF Analogy: The flow of delivery men walking along the road loop from warehouse to destination and back.
Meter: Ammeter (安培計) connected in Series (串聯 魚蛋串).
⚡ Unit: Volt / 伏特 (V)

Voltage (電壓)

The amount of electrical energy supplied by the cell to the electric charges to flow around the circuit.

SF Analogy: The amount of mail and parcels loaded onto each delivery man at the warehouse before setting off.
Meter: Voltmeter (伏特計) connected in Parallel (並聯 夾公仔).
🛑 Unit: Ohm / 歐姆 (Ω)

Resistance (電阻)

How difficult it is for the electric charges to flow through the electrical conductor.

SF Analogy: How difficult it is for the delivery man to walk through the road (muddy, bumpy road conditions).
Key Principle: Higher resistance opposes the flow of charges, slowing down the entire circuit (lowering Current).

How They Change With Each Other (物理量之間的相互關係)

1. More Voltage (更強電壓 / 更多乾電池):

Supplies more energy to charges ➔ Couriers carry more packages and push faster ➔ Current increases. (電壓增加，推動能量更大，電流增加。)

2. Higher Resistance (電阻增加 / 路段更加泥濘難行):

Greater difficulty for charges to flow ➔ Muddy road slows down couriers ➔ Current decreases. (電阻增加，阻礙變大，電流減少。)

Common Misconceptions in S2 Science (初二學生常見誤解澄清)

Understanding why intuitive "gut-feelings" about circuits are physically wrong!

❌ "Current is eaten up by the bulb"

Truth: Charges are never consumed! Couriers do not get eaten at the house; they drop off packages and return. Current before and after the bulb is identical!

❌ "Voltage and Current are the same"

Truth: Voltage is the energy supplied per charge (parcels per courier). Current is the flow of charges (couriers moving along the path).

❌ "Charges only slow down in the resistor"

Truth: Resistance slows down the entire loop equally, just like a single muddy road section holds up the entire delivery convoy!
