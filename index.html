Real Electric Circuit (真實電路世界)

Conventional Current (+ to −)
🔋 Cell (+ / −) = Voltage source 🔵 Charge (+q) = Recycled carrier ✨ Yellow Halo = Electrical Energy 💡 Bulb = Light energy release
Physical Truth: Electric charges are never consumed! They flow out from the positive (+) terminal with high energy, give up energy as light/heat at the bulb, and return to the negative (−) terminal.

Three Fundamental Properties of Electricity

In junior science, we master these three quantities using physical descriptions and laboratory units.

🌊 Unit: Ampere (A)

Current (電流)

The rate of flow of electric charges passing a point in the circuit per second.

SF Analogy: How many couriers run past a spot per minute (traffic flow).
Meter Used: Ammeter (安培計) connected in series.
⚡ Unit: Volt (V)

Voltage (電壓)

The electrical energy supplied by the cell to each unit of electric charge.

SF Analogy: How many mail packages are loaded onto each courier's backpack.
Meter Used: Voltmeter (伏特計) connected in parallel.
🛑 Unit: Ohm (Ω)

Resistance (電阻)

The opposition to the flow of electric charges through a material or component.

SF Analogy: How rough, muddy, or clogged the delivery road is.
Effect: Higher resistance reduces the flow of charges (lowers Current).

How They Change Together:

1. More Voltage (More Cells) ➔ Electric charges carry more energy, pushing a larger Current through the circuit.
2. More Resistance (Longer / Thinner Wire) ➔ Opposes charge motion more strongly, causing the Current to drop.

How to Measure: Authentic Hong Kong Lab Metaphors

Understanding why an Ammeter must be connected in Series, while a Voltmeter must be connected in Parallel.

🍢

Ammeter (安培計)

Measures Current (電流) in Ampere (A)

SERIES (串聯)
🍢 The Curry Fishball Metaphor (咖哩魚蛋串):

To skewer fishballs onto a bamboo stick, the fishballs must be threaded in one single continuous line.

Why break the wire?

Current is the flow rate of charges. To count every single charge, you must break the circuit and string the Ammeter directly inline so 100% of charges pass through it.

Symbol in Diagram: ───( A )─── (One unbroken loop)
🕹️

Voltmeter (伏特計)

Measures Voltage (電壓) in Volt (V)

PARALLEL (並聯)
🕹️ The Claw Machine Metaphor (夾公仔機):

Two claws reach down from outside and grip two distinct points across the toy.

Why DO NOT break the wire?

Voltage is the energy difference between before and after the appliance. You keep the circuit intact and clamp two conjunction leads across it.

Symbol in Diagram: ●───( V )───● (Two junction nodes)
